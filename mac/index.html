Markasso for Mac

The same fast, keyboard-first whiteboard, as a native Mac app.

Free · Apple Silicon and Intel · macOS 26 or later · beta

Download for Mac

Markasso isn't notarized yet: the first time, allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

What the Mac app adds

  • .markasso files as real documents: Finder, iCloud Drive, autosave and versions
  • Quick Look previews and Spotlight search
  • Native menus and shortcuts, tabs, Handoff, Share and Print
  • VoiceOver, light and dark, 8 languages
  • Cloud boards synced with the web, offline edits and conflict resolution (with Markasso Cloud, the same licence as the web)
  • An assistant preview with your own Anthropic or OpenAI key or a local model, which you can turn off
  • Automatic updates

Install

  1. Download and open the zip, then drag Markasso.app to Applications.
  2. Open it once: macOS says it cannot verify the developer.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Markasso and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. From then on it opens normally, and updates install themselves (Markasso > Check for Updates).
